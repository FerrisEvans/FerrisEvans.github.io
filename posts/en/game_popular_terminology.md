Stat names, abbreviations, and their effects vary from game to game. The tables below collect examples; check a game’s own descriptions for the exact meaning of each stat.

“Not specified” means that this reference does not give a full name; “No additional note” means that no further explanation is provided for that entry.

## Attributes

| Abbreviation | Meaning | Full name | Notes |
|:---:|:----------|:------------:|:-----------------------|
| STR | Strength | strength | Attack power, carrying capacity |
| VIT | Vitality, stamina | vitality | May affect health and stamina gained when leveling up; sometimes serves a similar role to constitution |
| CON | Constitution | constitution | No additional note |
| INT | Intelligence | intelligence | Magic attack power |
| WIZ | Wizardry, magic | wizardry | May affect magic attack power, much like INT |
| WIS | Wisdom, perception | wisdom | May affect mana or support magic |
| MND | Mind, mental strength | mind | Magic defense |
| SPI | Spirit | spirit | No additional note |
| MEN | Mental strength | mentality | No additional note |
| PIE | Piety | piety | Mana gained when leveling up; mana regeneration |
| DEX | Dexterity | dexterity | Attack speed, defense, evasion |
| SPD | Speed | speed | Movement speed, attack speed, casting speed, channeling speed |
| AGI | Agility | agility | May affect evasion or turn order |
| AGL | Agility | agility | Another abbreviation for agility; effects vary by game |
| LUK | Luck | luck | No additional note |
| PER | Personality | personality | No additional note |
| CHR | Charisma, charm | charisma | No additional note |
| KAR | Karma | karma | No additional note |

## Combat stats

| Abbreviation | Meaning | Full name | Notes |
|:-------------:|:---------------|:----------------:|:----------------------|
| HP | Health | hit points / health points | No additional note |
| MP/Mana | Mana | magic points / mana points | No additional note |
| SP | Skill points, skill energy, or stamina | Not specified | May be spent on abilities, used to unlock skills, or serve as an alternative to MP |
| CP | Rage or a special-move resource | Not specified | Game-specific; may need to reach a threshold before a special move becomes available |
| DAM | Damage | damage | No additional note |
| ATK | Physical attack power | attack | No additional note |
| MATK/MAG | Magic attack power | magic attack | No additional note |
| DEF | Physical defense | defense | No additional note |
| MDEF | Magic defense | magic defense | No additional note |
| AVO/AVD/AVOID | Evasion chance | Not specified | No additional note |
| RESIST/RES | Resistance | resistance | May apply to magic, elements, or status effects |
| WSP | Weapon attack speed | weapon speed | No additional note |
| CT/CR | Critical hit chance | critical hit rate | Abbreviation varies by game |
| RNG | Attack range | range | No additional note |
| CD | Cooldown | cooldown | No additional note |
| MOV/MOVP | Movement allowance | movement / movement points | How far a unit can move in one turn |
| HIT | Hit chance or hit count | hit | Meaning depends on the context |
| COMBO | Combo count | combo | A sequence of consecutive attacks or hits |
| SL | Slashing | slash | No additional note |
| TH | Thrusting | thrust | No additional note |
| EVA | Evasion | evasion | No additional note |
